A single line of plain text: a one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant. Entered in the [One Star for Monday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

> One star. I dropped my weekend in the tip jar and Monday kept the change.

`main.ts` writes that line to `entry.txt` in the output directory. Nothing else is written, read, or fetched.
